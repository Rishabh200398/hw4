Campus Customs — Live App Check

Problem 11 · Screenshots captured from the running app (frontend on :5173, FastAPI backend on :8000)

This page documents three checks run against the live Campus Customs site, each with a screenshot and a short note on what it proves. The data (prices and stock) comes from the local SQLite database campus_customs.db; the chatbot is a PydanticAI agent that reads that database through tools, so every price and stock figure it states is real.

Check 1 · Chatbot

Honest stock & price from the database

Handsome Dan (the chat assistant) is asked whether the Basic Hoodie Big Yale is available in Medium and its price. He answers “available in Medium, with 5 in stock. It’s $68 USD.” — read live from the inventory and catalogue tables (M = 5 units, price $68), not guessed.

Reproduce: open the chat (bottom-right) and ask “Is the Basic Hoodie Big Yale available in medium, and what is the price?”
Check 2 · Dynamic search

Category question → product cards appear on the page

Asking “what hoodies do you have?” in the chat makes the agent search the catalogue and the website renders the matching items as product cards under a “From your chat — Matches for …” band. The cards are clickable and each opens that product’s detail page.

Reproduce: open the chat and ask “what hoodies do you have?” — matching cards appear on the page.
Check 3 · Usability (Problem 9)

Filter the products by type and price

On the Products page, the Product type and Price dropdowns are checkbox filters. Here “Hoodies” is ticked; clicking Apply filters narrows the 102-item grid to just hoodies. This is one of the Problem 9 usability improvements (it combines with the search box, and a Clear button resets it).

Reproduce: go to Products → open “Product type” → check Hoodies → Apply filters (102 → 27 items).
